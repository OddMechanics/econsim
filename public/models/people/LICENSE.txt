People models and animations (people.glb, people-anim.bin)
==========================================================

Everyone is the same faceless, gender-neutral mannequin. The files are derived
(re-skinned, slimmed, recolored, retargeted) from two free asset packs by
Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), both released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain dedication):

1. Universal Animation Library (Standard / free version)
   https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
   https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
   Official page lists "License: CC0" ("Free to use in personal, educational
   and commercial projects. (CC0 License)"). Used: the Mannequin mesh and
   skeleton, and the clips Idle_Loop, Idle_Talking_Loop, Sitting_Idle_Loop,
   Sitting_Talking_Loop, Driving_Loop, Fixing_Kneeling, Walk_Loop,
   Walk_Formal_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop, Dance_Loop.

2. Ultimate Modular Men Pack (February 2022)
   https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
   Official page lists "License: CC0". Used: only the Idle, Idle_Neutral and
   Walk clips of the Casual_2 character (no meshes).

Licenses were checked on the official pages on 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24.
(Quaternius has since introduced a "Quaternius Asset License" for newer
releases; it states it does not apply retroactively, and the packs above are
still labeled CC0 on their pages. CC0 dedications cannot be revoked.)

Files were obtained from verbatim mirrors of the official downloads:
- https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library
  (glTF/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf + .bin: the free UAL glTF as
  distributed on itch.io; the repo states CC0 1.0)
- https://github.com/LuanDucate/Ducz.CharacterCreator
  (public/models/Male/Casual_2.glb: the official Individual Characters glTF
  file repacked to GLB, byte-identical data)

How the files were made: scripts/people-assets/build_people.py (run it with
the two downloads above). It maps the mannequin onto our 60-joint skeleton
(identity rest rotations), slims its sculpted shoulders, chest and arms toward
the bones for a neutral build, simplifies every piece to about a tenth of its
triangles (~1,000 per person; finger joint balls dropped), splits it into
head / body / legs / feet with clothes painted as palette zones per triangle
(welded, normals rebuilt flat at load), and retargets the clips
(world-rotation deltas, 30 fps, int16 quaternions). people-anim.bin is a small
custom format: "PANM", u32 version, u32 JSON length, JSON header (joints,
clips), then int16 frames (body position * 8192, 21 quaternions * 32767).
