Countryside models (country.glb)
================================

country.glb holds six small low-poly models, each one mesh of flat-shaded,
vertex-colored triangles in meters (standing on y = 0, facing +Z). They are
converted, scaled and (where noted) recolored from free asset packs released
under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):

  model      source                                                   changes
  ---------  -------------------------------------------------------  ------------------------------------------
  cow        Quaternius, "LowPoly Animated Farm Animal Pack" (2018),   OBJ -> vertex colors from its materials,
             OBJ/Cow.obj                                               static (no rig), scaled to 2.4 m long
  sheep      same pack, OBJ/Sheep.obj                                  same, scaled to 1.3 m long
  tractor    Kenney, "Car Kit" 3.1 (2026-04-02), GLB/tractor.glb       colormap texture baked to vertex colors,
                                                                       scaled to 3.9 m long
  ambulance  Kenney, "Car Kit" 3.1, GLB/ambulance.glb                  same, scaled to 5.4 m long
  fence      Kenney, "Nature Kit" 2.1 (2020-04-29), fence_simple.glb   scaled to a 3 m x 1.1 m section
  hedge      Kenney, "Nature Kit" 2.1, plant_bushLarge.glb             recolored mint -> hedge green, 2.2 m wide

Sources (official pages; each lists the CC0 license; checked 2026-10-24):
- Quaternius Farm Animal Pack: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-farm-animal-pack
  (download "Farm Animals by @Quaternius.zip"; its License.txt: "CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public
  Domain Dedication"). Also https://quaternius.com
- Kenney Car Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit (kenney_car-kit.zip; License.txt: "Creative Commons
  Zero, CC0")
- Kenney Nature Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit (kenney_nature-kit.zip; License.txt: "Creative
  Commons Zero, CC0")

How the file is made: scripts/build_country.py in the project (run it with the three
unzipped downloads; see its docstring). COLOR_0 holds linear colors as glTF
asks; the game converts them to display colors when it loads the file
(src/render/countryModels.ts).
